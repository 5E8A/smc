### :GaugeIcon: HUD & Overlays

Mods that put useful information directly on your screen, so you never have to guess.

#### :AppleLogoIcon: Appleskin

Ever wondered why your hunger bar has that flickering outline? Appleskin visualizes Minecraft's hidden food mechanics. Hold any food item to see exactly how much hunger and saturation it will restore, complete with a preview of how your hunger and health bars will change after eating. It also adds food values to item tooltips and hunger/saturation/exhaustion info to the debug screen (F3).

#### :ShieldCheckIcon: Durability Viewer

Displays your equipped armor pieces with their remaining durability in bottom right corner, so you know exactly when it is time to repair your gear.

Press **H** to show or hide the display - see [Keybinds and Controls](/smc/en/wiki/keybinds-and-controls).

![Durability Viewer showing armor in bottom right corner](/smc/assets/content/mods/qol/durability-viewer/armor-hotbar.webp "Armor durability displayed in bottom right corner")

#### :HeartbeatIcon: Health Indicator TXF

Adds a compact damage indicator to the HUD, letting you keep an eye on your health at all times without looking away from the action.

![Health Indicator TXF on the HUD](/smc/assets/content/mods/qol/health-indicator/hud.webp)

#### :LightbulbFilamentIcon: Lighty

Renders a colored overlay on block surfaces showing whether mobs can spawn there - green means safe, red means a mob could appear. Indispensable for lighting up bases and mob-proofing your builds. Toggle the overlay with **L**.

#### :WifiHighIcon: Ping View

Shows each player's ping as a number in the tab list, so you always know who is lagging - useful when playing on servers.

### :BasketIcon: Items & Inventory

Better ways to browse items, recipes and containers.

#### :BookOpenIcon: Just Enough Items

The classic recipe viewer. Every item in the game is one keystroke away: open your inventory and the item list appears on the right. Click any item to see how it is crafted, press **U** (or right-click) to see what it is used in, and press **R** on a result to jump back to its recipe. Supports search by name and mod with `@` filters.

:carouselStart:
![JEI recipe interface showing a crafting recipe](/smc/assets/content/mods/qol/jei/recipe-gui.webp)

![JEI item list edit mode](/smc/assets/content/mods/qol/jei/item-list-edit-mode.webp)
:carouselEnd:

#### :PackageIcon: Shulker Box Tooltip

Hover over any shulker box (or other container item) to preview its contents without placing it down. Great for organizing storage rooms and checking what is inside boxes in your ender chest or inventory. Multiple preview layouts are available in the config.

### :ChatsCircleIcon: Chat & Social

Tools that make multiplayer communication nicer and safer.

#### :UserCircleIcon: Chat Heads

Shows the skin head of each player next to their chat messages, so you can tell at a glance who is talking even when several people chat at once.

![Chat message rendering with player heads and drop shadow](/smc/assets/content/mods/qol/chat-heads/drop-shadow.webp)

#### :ChatCenteredDotsIcon: Talk Balloons

When you type in chat, your message also appears in a speech bubble above your character's head - perfect for communicating without opening the chat log.

:carouselStart:
![Talk Balloons speech bubble above a player](/smc/assets/content/mods/qol/talk-balloons/balloon.webp)
![Dark and gold styled chat balloons](/smc/assets/content/mods/qol/talk-balloons/qsmp-balloons.webp)
:carouselEnd:

#### :ClockCounterClockwiseIcon: More Chat History

Vanilla Minecraft only keeps the last 100 chat messages. This mod dramatically increases that limit, so you can scroll back through much longer conversations.

#### :ArrowUUpLeftIcon: Don't Clear Chat History

Normally your sent chat history is wiped whenever you relog or disconnect. This small tweak keeps it intact, so pressing up in chat still recalls everything you wrote.

#### :ShieldSlashIcon: No Chat Reports

Disables chat message signing, which makes your messages impossible to report through Minecraft's chat reporting system. Adds small status icons to the chat so you always know whether the server you are on is protected.

![No Chat Reports status icons in chat](/smc/assets/content/mods/qol/no-chat-reports/chat-icons.webp)

### :MountainsIcon: Visuals & Rendering

Squeeze more out of your graphics settings.

#### :BinocularsIcon: Bobby

Lets you see much further than the server's view distance allows. Bobby stores chunks the server has already sent you and keeps rendering them beyond the view-distance limit, effectively giving you a huge render distance on any server. Works great together with Nvidium (see [Performance Tweaks](/smc/en/wiki/performance-tweaks)).

#### :SunIcon: Gamma Utils

Raises the game brightness far beyond the vanilla slider. Toggle fullbright instantly with **G** - perfect for caving without torch-spamming. The gamma slider goes up to 1500% and optional night vision and smooth transitions are available in the config.

:carouselStart:
![Comparison between 100% and 1500% gamma](/smc/assets/content/mods/qol/gamma-utils/comparison.webp)
![Client-side night vision combined with shaders](/smc/assets/content/mods/qol/gamma-utils/night-vision.webp)
![Optional gamma status effect shown on the HUD](/smc/assets/content/mods/qol/gamma-utils/status-effect.webp)
:carouselEnd:

#### :CloudSunIcon: Smooth Skies

At high render distances the vanilla skybox shows a harsh line between the blue sky and the white horizon fog. Smooth Skies blends those colors into a clean gradient and also fixes sky rendering issues at low render distances.

### :LightningIcon: Loading & Stability

Get into your world faster and survive crashes gracefully.

#### :SignOutIcon: Fast Quit

Quitting a world used to freeze the game until the save finished. With Fast Quit the save runs in the background while you return straight to the main menu - you can even rejoin the same world immediately.

#### :SkipForwardIcon: EpicForceCloseLoadingScreen

Cuts the "Loading terrain..." screen the instant your world is ready instead of waiting for the animation, shaving seconds off every single world join.

#### :HourglassIcon: Remove Reloading Screen

Resource pack reloads normally lock up the whole screen with a loading overlay. This mod makes the reload happen behind a translucent overlay so you can keep watching your game while packs load.

#### :BugIcon: Not Enough Crashes

When the game crashes, you no longer lose everything. Instead of dumping you to the launcher, Not Enough Crashes catches the error, lets you return to the title screen (or straight back into your world) and keeps playing. It also produces detailed crash reports that point at the offending mod.

### :UsersThreeIcon: Account & Online

Accounts, friends and playing together.

#### :SealCheckIcon: Auth Me

If your Microsoft session expires, Auth Me lets you re-authenticate your account from inside the game - including offline-mode accounts - without restarting Minecraft or fiddling with the launcher.

An all-in-one social layer: add friends, send DMs, host your singleplayer worlds for others to join (see [World Hosting](/smc/en/wiki/world-hosting)), manage skins with the wardrobe, take and share enhanced screenshots, and customize your look with free cosmetics.

### :MapTrifoldIcon: Map & Navigation

Never get lost again.

#### :MapTrifoldIcon: VoxelMap

A full mapping suite: a minimap in the corner of your screen, a fullscreen world map (**M**), and unlimited waypoints (**N**) that render in-world so you can navigate home, to that village, or to your friend's base. Zoom the minimap with **Z** and toggle the fullscreen map with **X**.

More keybinds can be found in [Keybinds and Controls](/smc/en/wiki/keybinds-and-controls).
